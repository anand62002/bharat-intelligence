## Abstract

arXiv:2604.25931v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: We identify a previously unknown calibration property of large language models: providing one confirmed intermediate fact toward a multi-step reasoning chain increases the model's confident-wrong-answer rate before full evidence eliminates it. We call this anchored confabulation: a partial anchor commits the model to confident parametric completion of remaining reasoning steps. We formalize it as Parametric Hallucination Confidence (PHC) and establish it across six lines of evidence including a causal injection experiment (PHC 0.613 to 0.656 to 0.595 to 0.536, N=160) and capability scaling across five model families (Spearman rho=0.900, p=0.037).
