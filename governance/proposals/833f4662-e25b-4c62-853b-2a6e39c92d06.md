## Proposed Change

Implement GraphRAG alongside existing VectorRAG in agents/historical_rag.py by adding a knowledge graph layer that connects stock entities (companies, sectors, events, financial metrics) with relationships extracted from historical data. Modify the retrieval function to perform dual-path lookup: pgvector similarity search (existing) + graph traversal for multi-hop reasoning (new), then merge results before sending to LLM.
