## Abstract

arXiv:2608.05234v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Building reliable applications that leverage large language models (LLMs) remains a significant challenge. While LLMs offer impressive capabilities across diverse tasks, their outputs often lack accuracy and provide no clear measure of confidence. This uncertainty compounds in flows of multiple calls to LLMs and other tools, making it difficult for developers and end-users to trust the results. This paper introduces a probabilistic language for programming LLM-based flows. It enables developers to quantify and propagate uncertainty throughout the application's flow, and experiment with different inference scaling techniques without adding a single line of code beyond the flow's logic.
