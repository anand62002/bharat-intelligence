## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) can synthesize financial narratives but may express high confidence when evidence is sparse, stale, or contradictory. This failure is especially consequential in forecasting, where filings, news, prices, volume, and technical signals can disagree. We present FinAbstain, a research framework for uncertainty-calibrated multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with selective prediction. A point-in-time retriever admits only information public at the forecast timestamp and supplies modality-specific evidence to fundamental, news, technical, risk, and verification agents. ## Proposed Change

Add an uncertainty-calibrated abstention mechanism to the LangGraph orchestrator (orchestrator/synthesizer.py). After collecting all 7 agent signals, compute a composite uncertainty score combining: (1) signal disagreement variance, (2) data freshness penalties (age > 24h), (3) confidence score standard deviation across agents, and (4) historical calibration error from performance_tracker. If uncertainty exceeds a threshold (initially 0.65, tunable via A/B test), return signal='ABSTAIN' with explanation instead of forcing BUY/HOLD/SELL, preventing overconfident recommendations when evidence conflicts.
