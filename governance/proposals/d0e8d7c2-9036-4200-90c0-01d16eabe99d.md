## Abstract

arXiv:2610.21731v1 Announce Type: cross 
Abstract: Web search allows LLMs to access up-to-date information, but it remains unclear why they sometimes produce outdated answers even when relevant evidence is available. To study this problem, we construct temporal facts whose values changed after the models' training cutoffs. We evaluate one proprietary model under deployed web search and six open-weight model configurations under controlled retrieval. We find that models often answer from search snippets without opening the source pages, which is associated with higher rates of outdated answers and unfaithful citations. ## Proposed Change

Add evidence utilization tracking to agents/historical_rag.py by implementing a two-stage verification: (1) log which retrieved documents were actually cited in the LLM's final response using citation parsing, (2) add a post-synthesis check in orchestrator/synthesizer.py that re-queries the LLM with explicit instructions to quote specific retrieved facts, comparing answers with/without this prompting.
