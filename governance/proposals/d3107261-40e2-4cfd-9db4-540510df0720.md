Abstract: In RAG, while the retriever typically ranks documents by their individual relevance to the query, the generator instead produces an answer based on the retrieved documents as a whole. ## Proposed Change

Modify agents/historical_rag.py's retrieval function to implement diversity-aware re-ranking after initial pgvector similarity search. After retrieving top-20 candidates by embedding similarity, apply Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR) algorithm to select final top-5 documents that balance relevance and diversity (λ=0.7), avoiding redundant paraphrased content from the same source/date. This addresses the paper's finding that diverse document sets outperform duplicate/paraphrased sets for generation quality.
