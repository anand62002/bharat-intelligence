## Abstract

arXiv:2611.00515v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used in dynamic financial trading, yet real-time trading benchmarks mainly evaluate agents by cumulative returns and risk-adjusted performance. Such outcome metrics cannot show whether trades are faithfully reported, supported by available evidence, and consistent with account states. We introduce AStockArena, a real-time decision-auditing benchmark for continuous trading in China's A-share market. Under shared market conditions and execution constraints, we evaluate 13 open- and closed-source models over 3,198 sequential decisions. ## Proposed Change

Add a TradingAuditLayer class in orchestrator/synthesis.py that implements AStockArena's three-pillar audit checks before final recommendations. For each synthesized signal, verify (O) execution traceability by logging agent source chains and timestamps, (RK) evidence sufficiency by requiring ≥2 data sources per claim with confidence scores, and (B) financial meta-awareness by validating account state constraints (cash available, position limits). Reject or downgrade signals failing ≥2 checks and log audit gaps to Supabase for weekly performance_tracker analysis.
