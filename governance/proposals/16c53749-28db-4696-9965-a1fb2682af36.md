## Abstract

Abstract: Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lets a large language model (LLM) answer questions by retrieving relevant passages from a large corpus. However, RAG struggles with multi-hop questions, which require chaining facts spread across several documents through intermediate entities that the question never mentions. Graph-based RAG methods retrieve once from the entities a question names and so rarely recover the full evidence chain. Iterative RAG methods recover it step by step, but their accuracy is limited by the many tasks they ask the LLM to perform, such as planning the sub-questions, deciding their dependencies, rewriting them as answers arrive, and keeping track of what is known. ## Proposed Change

Refactor agents/historical_rag.py to implement DeFT-RAG's incremental decomposition strategy. Replace the current single-shot vector similarity retrieval with a loop that: (1) generates one self-contained sub-question from the original query plus accumulated QA pairs, (2) performs pgvector retrieval on that sub-question with explicit entity names, (3) appends the answer to the QA chain, (4) repeats until sufficient context is gathered. This addresses multi-hop queries like 'Which Nifty 500 stock with improving promoter holding also benefited from recent FII inflows?' by breaking it into 'Which stocks show increasing promoter holding?' then 'Among [stock_list], which had net FII buying in last 30 days?'
