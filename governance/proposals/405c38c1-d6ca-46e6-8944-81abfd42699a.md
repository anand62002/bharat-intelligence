## Abstract

arXiv:2611.08498v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: The vast amount of information available in lengthy, distributed documents makes finding relevant answers difficult, especially when the answer requires connecting evidence from multiple sources. Standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems often struggle with this type of "multi-hop" reasoning and frequently face issues with context fragmentation. In this paper, we propose a hybrid RAG framework that integrates the structured reasoning of knowledge graphs with the broad semantic coverage of dense vector retrieval. Our approach employs a multi-stage pipeline using transformer-based summarization and semantic chunking to improve context retention.
