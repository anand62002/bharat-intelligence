## Abstract

arXiv:2610.02351v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: ReAct-based agents typically rely on a single LLM policy to propose actions, interact with the environment, and decide when a task is complete. This coupling makes action authorization and completion control difficult to enforce independently, allowing errors to propagate and unsupported completion claims to terminate execution. We introduce DeReAct, a modular agent architecture that externalizes two gating policies: a Critic that validates proposed actions before execution, and a Context Manager that reconstructs an environment-supported \textsc{State} and certifies task completion.
  Across GAIA and SWE-bench Verified, DeReAct improves Pass@1 most for weaker Brain models, with gains of 6.5--7.0 points for Qwen3-Coder-480B and 4.2--5.2 poin

## Proposed Change

Implement DeReAct-style external gating in orchestrator/langgraph_synthesizer.py by adding a CriticNode that validates each agent's proposed signal (BUY/SELL/HOLD/AVOID) before synthesis. The CriticNode uses Claude Haiku to verify: (1) signal-score alignment (e.g., BUY must have score >70), (2) data_sources match detail claims, (3) no conflicting indicators within detail dict. Add CompletionManagerNode that verifies final synthesis has 5+ agent inputs and checks contradictory signals are addressed in synthesis rationale before returning recommendation.
