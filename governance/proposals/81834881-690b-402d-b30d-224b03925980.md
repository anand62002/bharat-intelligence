## Abstract

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are often used in conjunction with external knowledge sources to improve their factual accuracy and decrease hallucinations, through methods such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). However, these systems remain susceptible to intrinsic hallucinations, where the model generates unfaithful or fabricated information that is not supported by the retrieved evidence. We propose a novel framework to assess model robustness against this phenomenon by stress-testing using natural, semantically equivalent variations of a user query found via adversarial optimization methods.
