## Abstract

Abstract: A retrieval-augmented language model answers from two sources, the retrieved context and its own knowledge, and the two disagree whenever the context is incomplete or wrong. Existing methods settle the disagreement with a rule fixed at design time: follow the context, amplify it in proportion to how far it moves the model, or trust the model's memory. ## Proposed Change

In orchestrator/synthesizer.py, replace the current fixed-rule conflict resolution (likely a weighted average or 'trust context' heuristic) with a Bayesian prior-based system. Add a context_reliability_tracker that maintains per-source reliability priors (e.g., P(Screener.in correct) = 0.92, P(RSS sentiment correct) = 0.68) updated weekly via performance_tracker outcomes. When agents disagree (e.g., fundamental says BUY score=85, sentiment says SELL score=70), compute posterior probabilities using each agent's historical accuracy as prior × current confidence, then weight final signal by posteriors instead of naive averaging.
