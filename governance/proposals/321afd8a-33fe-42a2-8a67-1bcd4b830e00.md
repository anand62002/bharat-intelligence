## Proposed Change

Add a new ExecutionReality class in orchestrator/synthesis.py that enforces point-in-time discipline by (1) rejecting any agent signal that references data timestamped after market close on decision day, (2) logging actual NSE close prices at signal generation time vs. assumed execution prices, (3) applying realistic slippage (0.05% for liquid Nifty 500, 0.15% for mid/small-cap) and fixed brokerage (₹20/trade) to all BUY/SELL signals before passing to synthesis, and (4) tracking turnover and recording monthly transaction cost burden. This directly addresses the paper's core finding that execution timing and friction assumptions are underreported yet materially compress trading results.
