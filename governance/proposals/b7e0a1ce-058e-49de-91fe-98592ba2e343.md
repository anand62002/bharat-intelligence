## Abstract

arXiv:2610.30435v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Recent advances in search agents have enabled large language models to tackle complex tasks by retrieving and synthesizing external information. However, existing search agents often lack search-state awareness, i.e., the ability to recognize what information has been obtained and what remains missing, leading to hallucinated reasoning and prematurely terminated search. To address this limitation, we propose Self-Assessment Reinforcement Learning (SARL), a reinforcement learning framework in which models learn to recognize their own search state during training and improve their search behavior accordingly, without requiring additional assessment at test time. Implement search-state awareness in historical_rag agent (agents/historical_rag.py) by adding a self-assessment mechanism that tracks: (1) which embeddings/time periods have been queried, (2) confidence scores for retrieved context, and (3) identified information gaps.
