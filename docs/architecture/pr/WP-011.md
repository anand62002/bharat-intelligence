## WP-011 — Run the regime detector before the orchestrator

### Why
The regime job ran at 06:30 IST, half an hour after the 06:00 orchestrator. The orchestrator's
`load_weights_node` reads the latest `market_regime` row, so every run used yesterday's regime.
(Regime weight multipliers are currently off under ADR-003, but the regime is still logged and
passed through state, and will matter again if multipliers are re-enabled.)

### What changed
- `worker.py`: regime detector trigger 06:30 → **05:45 IST**, after the 05:30 morning digest and
  15 minutes before the orchestrator. Module docstring, job docstring/log line and the start-up
  schedule log updated. The start-up log also listed the morning digest at its old 08:45 time;
  now 05:30.
- `CLAUDE.md`: worker schedule block and the regime_detector line.
- `api/main.py`: one docstring said 06:30 (outside `touches`; text only).
- New `tests/test_worker_schedule.py` (4): builds the scheduler without starting it and checks,
  from the triggers, that the regime job fires at 05:45 and before the orchestrator on every day
  of a week, and that the morning digest still precedes the orchestrator.
