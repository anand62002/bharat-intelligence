## WP-006 — Alert when a job is missing or breaks its invariant (ADR-005)

### Why
The portfolio pipeline ran and produced nothing for months while reporting success. WP-005's ledger
records that a job ran; this package checks what it **produced** and raises an alert when a job is
missing, failed, or produced nothing. This is mechanism 2 of ADR-005.

### What changed
- **New `scheduler/invariants.py`**: a declarative list of `Invariant(job, days, check, why)`.
  - `days` is `"daily"` or `"trading"`. Trading-day checks are skipped on weekends, and on weekdays with no Nifty 50 bar (exchange holidays). If that lookup fails, the day is assumed open.
  - Each job's latest row for the day is judged MISSING, FAILED, or OUTPUT (ran, but produced the wrong thing).
  - Rules:

    | Job | Days | OUTPUT breach when |
    |---|---|---|
    | orchestrator | daily | status not OK/WARNING, or 0 recommendations while holdings exist |
    | discovery | trading | status not OK |
    | forward_poller | trading | repriced < 90% of polled open recs |
    | paper_portfolio_update | trading | no snapshot saved, or 0 open positions updated |
    | discovery_rescorer | trading | every live call failed its re-check |
    | outcome_tracker | daily | (row must exist and not FAIL; zero due is normal) |
    | target_updater | trading | reviewed 0 holdings while holdings exist |

  - `check_invariants(client, run_date)` never raises: an unreadable `daily_runs` is itself reported as a `watchdog` breach.
  - Also adds `check_range()` (multi-day), `raise_alerts()` (one CRITICAL `portfolio_alerts` row per breach, `alert_type JOB_INVARIANT`, de-duplicated per job per day by title) and `is_trading_day()`.
- **`worker.py`: `job_watchdog` at 19:00 IST**, after the last daily job at 18:30. It is itself tracked in the ledger, and a run with breaches records WARNING.
- **`api/main.py`: `/api/system/health`** gains a "Job invariants" check covering yesterday, the last complete day, plus a `job_breaches` array. It uses a weekday calendar so the endpoint stays DB-only.
- **`scripts/weekly_audit.py`: new `job_invariants` check** over the last 7 days, ending yesterday. Any breach is a FAIL.
- **Tests: `tests/test_invariants.py` (24).**
  - The cases the plan names: missing, failed and zero-output each give exactly one breach; a healthy day gives none; weekends and holidays skip trading-day jobs.
  - Each per-job rule.
  - The latest row wins, so a rerun clears a crash.
  - An unreadable `daily_runs` becomes a breach; a rule that raises becomes a breach.
  - Range order and the trading calendar.
  - Alert insert, de-duplication, and never raising.
  - The offline drill: one job missing gives exactly one JOB_INVARIANT alert and a WARNING watchdog row.
  - The 19:00 schedule, and every invariant naming a real job.

### Risk
Medium.
- CRITICAL alerts appear in the governance banner and in `/api/portfolio/alerts` (the Portfolio tab), as intended. The first days after deploy may surface real gaps that were silent before.
- Context counts (open holdings) are read at check time. For past days in the weekly audit they're today's numbers, and the breach text says so implicitly.
- The trading calendar makes one yfinance call per past weekday checked by the watchdog or audit. A failed call assumes the day was open (a false alarm rather than a missed one).

### Rollback
Remove the 19:00 `job_watchdog` from `worker.py`. Alerts already written can be resolved in bulk:
`update portfolio_alerts set resolved = true where alert_type = 'JOB_INVARIANT';`
